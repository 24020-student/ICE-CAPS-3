ACT

What are other people doing to help this issue and how can you assist as well?

Learn

The first thing you should do is learn more about the world around you so you have context to understand the devastating effects of climate change.

Advocate

Everyone has the ability to stand up for issues they care about and so can you! Start or go to an environmental protest with friends to raise awareness for an important cause.

Inform

The most important environmental action is to inform yourself and others. This is so that we can all make more environmentally friendly descisions.

Research

Another way you can help to conserve the ice caps is to work on research so we can improve our efforts to aid the ice caps and our knowledge on them in general.

Overall, the best thing you can do is anything that you can actually do! Every action you take brings us closer to a better environment with lower sea levels, more ice caps, and beautiful icy habitats for our treasured animals.
